## 📝 Step-by-Step: Create a Blog Post

### Step 1: Open Your Studio

Go to: **https://roamingwheeels.sanity.studio/**

Sign in with your Sanity account (same one you used to deploy)

---

### Step 2: Create New Post

1. Click **"Blog Post"** in the left sidebar
2. Click **"+ Create"** button (top right)

---

### Step 3: Fill in the Form

#### Required Fields:

**1. Title**
```
Example: "Riding Through the Sahara Desert"
```

**2. Slug**
- Click the **"Generate"** button
- Creates URL: `riding-through-the-sahara-desert`

**3. Excerpt** (max 200 characters)
```
Example: "Three weeks, 3000km, and countless lessons 
learned from the world's largest desert."
```

**4. Featured Image**
- Click **Upload** or drag & drop
- Add **Alt Text**: `"Motorcycle in Sahara dunes"`

**5. Category** (choose one)
- Adventures
- Challenges
- Culture
- Tips
- Reflections

**6. Location**
```
Example: "Sahara Desert, Morocco"
```

**7. Content** (your blog post)

Use the editor:

```
[Heading 2] My Journey Begins

The morning sun was already blazing as I packed 
my motorcycle. Today would mark the start of my 
Sahara crossing.

[Add Image] motorcycle-packed.jpg
Caption: "Ready for the desert"

[Heading 2] Day 1: Into the Dunes

The first day was harder than expected...

• Pack light
• Carry extra water
• Trust your navigation

[Quote] "The desert teaches patience like nothing else."

[Heading 2] What I Learned

After 21 days in the Sahara, here are my key takeaways:

1. Preparation is everything
2. Local knowledge is invaluable
3. Respect the desert's power
```

**8. Featured Post**
- Toggle **ON** for important posts (shows bigger on homepage)
- Toggle **OFF** for regular posts

**9. Read Time**
```
Example: 8
```
(minutes to read)

**10. Published At**
- Auto-filled with current date
- Can change to schedule for later

---

### Step 4: Publish

Click the green **"Publish"** button (bottom right)

**Done!** Your post is now live! 🎉

---

## 🖼️ Adding Images to Your Post

**In the content editor:**

1. Click where you want the image
2. Click the **image icon** in toolbar
3. Upload your image
4. Add:
   - **Caption**: Shows below image
   - **Alt text**: For accessibility
5. Image appears in your post!

## ✏️ Rich Text Editor Tools

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Normal** dropdown | Change to H1, H2, H3, H4, Quote |
| **B** | Make text bold |
| ***I*** | Make text italic |
| `<>` | Format as code |
| **•** | Create bullet list |
| **1.** | Create numbered list |
| **🔗** | Add link to text |
| **📷** | Insert image |

## ✏️ Editing Published Posts

1. Go to https://roamingwheeels.sanity.studio/

3. Find your post in the list
4. Click to open it
5. Make changes
6. Click **"Publish"** again

Changes appear on website immediately!

After publishing:

1. Go to your website (local or Cloudflare Pages)

## 💡 Pro Tips

✅ **Save Drafts** - Click "Save Draft" to save without publishing  
✅ **Use H2 Headings** - Break up long posts into sections  
✅ **Add Images** - Visual content is more engaging  
✅ **Write Good Excerpts** - This is what people see first  
✅ **Check Preview** - Always preview before publishing  
✅ **Alt Text** - Add to all images for accessibility  
✅ **Featured Posts** - Use sparingly for your best content

## 🆘 Need Help?

**Can't remember URL?**
- It's: https://roamingwheeels.sanity.studio/
- Bookmark it!

**Forgot password?**
- Click "Forgot password" on login screen
- Or login with Google/GitHub

**Post not showing on website?**
- Make sure it's Published (not Draft)
- Check publication date isn't in future
- Wait 30 seconds and refresh
- Clear browser cache

**Want to delete a post?**
- Open the post
- Click menu (three dots)
- Choose "Delete"
- Confirm
